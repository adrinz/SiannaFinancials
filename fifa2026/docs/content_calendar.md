## How to use this calendar

- **Format per day:** Hook (first 1–2 seconds / text overlay) → Reel script (scene-by-scene) → On-screen captions → CTA → Hashtags.
- **Length:** Keep reels 15–30s. Hook in the first second or you lose 60% of viewers.
- **3-timezone posting windows (post the SAME reel 1–2x to catch all regions):**
  - 🌏 Asia: 12:30 PM IST / 8:00 PM JST
  - 🇪🇺 Europe: 7:00–9:00 PM CET
  - 🌎 Americas: 12:00 PM ET + 7:00 PM ET (catches LATAM evening)
- **Regional angle tag** is included per day so you can localize captions (swap the lead team/player for the region you're posting to).
- **Audio:** Use trending audio for hot takes/reactions; use cinematic/tension audio for predictions and knockout content.
- **Always pin a question in the comments** — comments are the #1 ranking signal for football reels.

## Content engine cheat-sheet (reuse forever)

**Hook formulas that print engagement:**
1. "Say it with me: ___" (controversial agreement bait)
2. "Screenshot this." (accountability / save bait)
3. "Nobody is talking about ___" (insider angle)
4. "This is the [best/worst] ___ of the tournament." (ranking bait)
5. "Fight me / Cope in the comments." (rage bait — use sparingly)
6. "Did we just witness ___?" (shared-moment bait)

**Reaction-content workflow (turn matches into reels in <20 min):**
1. During the game, screenshot 3 key moments + final score graphic.
2. Pick ONE angle: hero, villain, controversy, or shock.
3. Record a 20s talking-head or voiceover over the screenshots.
4. Post within 60 min of full-time (recency = reach for match content).

**Engagement-bait questions to pin (rotate):**
- "Wrong answers only: who wins the World Cup?"
- "One word to describe that game 👇"
- "Underrated player nobody's mentioning?"
- "GOAT debate — settled or never-ending?"

**Compliance note:** Use your own footage, licensed clips, or graphics/stills you have rights to. Replays of broadcast match footage can trigger copyright takedowns — recreate big moments with graphics, tactic-board animations, or fan-cam content where possible.

**Hashtag base set (mix 3–5 per post):**
`#WorldCup2026 #FIFA26 #football #soccer #worldcup #futbol` + 1–2 niche tags from the day.
